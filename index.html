Popular houseplant

Monstera Deliciosa

Our best-selling statement plant — glossy, fenestrated leaves that get more dramatic with every new growth. Ships in a nursery pot, ready to repot or display as-is.

☀ Bright, indirect light 💧 Water weekly ⚠ Toxic to pets Repot every 2 years

Care specs

Light Bright, indirect — avoid harsh direct sun
Watering Every 7–10 days; let the top 2 inches dry out
Toxicity Toxic to cats and dogs if chewed — keep out of reach
Repotting Size up every 18–24 months, spring is ideal

Our story

We started with one windowsill and a stubborn belief that plants belong indoors.

Verdanta began in a one-bedroom apartment that had quietly turned into a greenhouse. What started as a hobby of trading cuttings with neighbors grew into a mission: help people bring nature indoors without the guesswork — matching every plant to the light, the routine, and the household it's actually going into, pets included.

Sourcing partners, Rajshahi
How we source

Eco-friendly packaging, responsible cultivation

Every plant is grown by small nurseries we've worked with for years, shipped in compostable packaging with recycled paper padding — no plastic peanuts, no single-use foam. We turn away suppliers who over-harvest or rely on heavy pesticide use, even when it costs us a faster restock.

  • Compostable, plastic-free packaging on every order
  • Nursery partners audited for responsible growing practices
  • Cuttings and divisions prioritized over wild collection
